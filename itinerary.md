# Open-Source Roadshow 2026 — Detailed Itinerary

A four-city van tour in spring 2026: Paris → Berlin → Milan → Barcelona. Each evening meetup takes place at a famous landmark. Attendees are encouraged to arrive by train; the roadshow crew drives the van between cities.

## Stop 1 — Paris (Eiffel Tower)

- **Venue:** Eiffel Tower
- **Address:** Av. Gustave Eiffel, 75007 Paris, France
- **Venue details:** Rating 4.7 (about 498,600 reviews); open daily 9:00 AM – 12:00 AM. Gustave Eiffel's iconic wrought-iron 1889 tower with steps and elevators to observation decks.
- **Station connection:** Gare du Nord → Eiffel Tower: 6.4 km, about 26 minutes by car.
- **Welcome notice (French):**

> Bienvenue à notre tournée de présentation open source ! Rejoignez-nous pour une soirée de présentations, de démonstrations en direct et de hacking communautaire. Venez avec votre ordinateur portable et vos questions : nous vous aiderons à réaliser votre première contribution. À bientôt !

## Stop 2 — Berlin (Brandenburg Gate)

- **Driving leg:** Paris → Berlin: 1,059 km, about 11 hours 17 minutes.
- **Venue:** Brandenburg Gate
- **Address:** Pariser Platz, 10117 Berlin, Germany
- **Venue details:** Rating 4.7 (about 191,300 reviews); a public landmark, accessible at all hours. Restored 18th-century gate with 12 Doric columns topped by a classical goddess statue.
- **Station connection:** Berlin Hauptbahnhof → Brandenburg Gate: 2.9 km, about 10 minutes by car.
- **Welcome notice (German):**

> Herzlich willkommen zu unserer Open-Source-Roadshow! Kommt vorbei zu einem Abend voller Vorträge, Live-Demos und gemeinsamen Hackens mit der Community. Bringt euren Laptop und eure Fragen mit – wir helfen euch dabei, euren ersten Beitrag zu leisten. Wir freuen uns auf euch!

## Stop 3 — Milan (Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II)

- **Driving leg:** Berlin → Milan: 1,034 km, about 11 hours 20 minutes.
- **Venue:** Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II
- **Address:** 20123 Milan, Metropolitan City of Milan, Italy
- **Venue details:** Rating 4.7 (about 116,700 reviews); open 24 hours. A glass-covered 19th-century arcade with luxury brands and upscale dining. Phone: +39 02 8845 5555.
- **Station connection:** Milano Centrale → Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II: 3.8 km, about 16 minutes by car.
- **Welcome notice (Italian):**

> Benvenuti al nostro roadshow open-source! Unitevi a noi per una serata di interventi, demo dal vivo e hacking di comunità. Porta il tuo laptop e le tue domande: ti aiuteremo a realizzare il tuo primo contributo. Ci vediamo lì!

## Stop 4 — Barcelona (Sagrada Família)

- **Driving leg:** Milan → Barcelona: 982 km, about 10 hours 15 minutes.
- **Venue:** Basilica of the Sagrada Familia
- **Address:** Carrer de Mallorca, 401, Eixample, 08013 Barcelona, Spain
- **Venue details:** Rating 4.8 (about 336,900 reviews); Monday–Friday 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM, Sunday 10:30 AM – 8:00 PM. Antoni Gaudí's renowned unfinished church, started in the 1880s, with museum and city views. Phone: +34 932 08 04 14.
- **Station connection:** Barcelona Sants → Sagrada Família: 7.6 km, about 18 minutes by car.
- **Welcome notice (Spanish):**

> ¡Os damos la bienvenida a nuestro roadshow de código abierto! Acompáñanos en una velada con charlas, demostraciones en directo y hacking en comunidad. Trae tu portátil y tus preguntas: te ayudaremos a hacer tu primera contribución. ¡Nos vemos allí!
