# Open-Source Roadshow 2026

The first contributor roadshow for our open-source project — a four-city van tour across Europe in spring 2026, with a free evening meetup at a famous landmark in each city: talks, live demos, and community hacking. Newcomers welcome — bring a laptop and your questions, and we will help you land your first contribution.

## The tour

| Stop | City | Venue |
|------|------|-------|
| 1 | Paris | Eiffel Tower |
| 2 | Berlin | Brandenburg Gate |
| 3 | Milan | Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II |
| 4 | Barcelona | Sagrada Família |

The roadshow crew drives the van between cities; attendees are encouraged to travel by train. The full itinerary — venue addresses, opening details, station connections, driving legs, and the welcome notice in each local language — is in `itinerary.md`.

See you there!
